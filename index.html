WOF
02

Warrant of Fitness (WOF)

Under New Zealand law, vehicles require regular Warrant of Fitness safety checks. Our authorized inspectors perform efficient, comprehensive inspections to ensure all safety standards are met.

  • NZTA Certified Inspection: Official safety warrant evaluations.
  • Brakes & Suspension Check: Critical stopping power assessment.
  • Lights & Visibility: Checking headlights, indicators, and wipers.
  • WOF Repairs: Instant fixes and re-testing if your vehicle fails.
Inspection rate $65

Vehicle Repair & Diagnostics

  • Suspension & Steering: Struts, shocks, CV joints, and ball joints repairs.
